# ERP and Operations Platform

## Problem

An operations-heavy business needed one system for workflow, master data, transactions, reporting and role-based access instead of a fragmented spreadsheet and legacy-software process.

## My contribution

I designed and built the platform end to end: domain workflows, database model, API layer, web interface, reports, deployment concerns and operational support patterns.

## Technical proof

- Full-stack business application design
- Relational data modelling and transaction workflows
- Role-based access and audit-friendly operations
- Nuxt/Vue front end with API-backed modules
- Practical reporting and maintainable deployment boundaries

## Outcome

The system became a production business platform for complex operational work. Public details are intentionally anonymised; the appropriate interview discussion is the architecture, trade-offs, testing approach and delivery ownership.

## Evidence boundary

No private client name, internal URL, credential or confidential business metric is published here. A tailored interview walkthrough can use a sanitized architecture diagram and representative workflow data.
